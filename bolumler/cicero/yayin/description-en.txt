The spy who was paid in fake money: Cicero

Elyesa Bazna, code name Cicero, was valet to the British ambassador in Ankara in 1943–44. He is said to have photographed the ambassador's secret papers and sold them to Moyzisch of the German embassy. Most of the roughly £300,000 he was paid is said to have been forged sterling from Germany's Operation Bernhard (how much was fake varies by source). How much Berlin used is also disputed; Britannica says most of it, Normandy included, was disregarded. Details such as the wax impression, the bathroom and the car handovers come from Bazna's and Moyzisch's own accounts, both self-interested. Scenes and gestures are dramatized.
